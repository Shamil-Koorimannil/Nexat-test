Company Overview
NEXAT Construction Co. is a Saudi-owned multidisciplinary engineering, construction, and real estate development company providing comprehensive EPC, Design & Build, and General Contracting solutions across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.
Vision
To become the Middle East's most trusted engineering and construction partner, recognized for delivering landmark projects through innovation, quality, and sustainable excellence.
Mission
To provide integrated engineering and construction solutions that create lasting value through technical expertise, operational excellence, safety leadership, and continuous innovation.
Core Values
Integrity, Safety First, Engineering Excellence, Client Partnership, Accountability, Sustainability, Innovation, Continuous Improvement, Respect, Quality Without Compromise.
Why Choose NEXAT
Engineering-led solutions, turnkey delivery, international standards, value engineering, fast mobilization, sustainable construction.
Business Divisions
General Contracting; Civil & Infrastructure; Structural Engineering; Architectural Finishing & Interior Fit-Out; MEP Engineering; Waterproofing & Thermal Insulation; Real Estate Development; Facility Management.
Market Sectors
Government, Commercial, Residential, Healthcare, Hospitality, Education, Industrial, Logistics, Infrastructure, Energy, Mixed-Use Developments.
Project Delivery Methodology
Client Consultation → Feasibility Study → Engineering & Design → Value Engineering → Procurement → Construction → QA/QC → Testing & Commissioning → Handover → Facility Management.
